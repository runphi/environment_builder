- preempt-rt: The patch enable the fully preemption mode in the kernel (https://wiki.linuxfoundation.org/realtime/start). It is 6.1.69-rt21 adapted to linux-xlnx 6.1.70 (xlnx_rebase_v6.1_LTS); the header of the patch says what was changed. Enable PREEMPT_RT (needs EXPERT) in the kernel defconfig as well.
- kvm_stage2_free_flush: KVM invalidates the TLBs of all VMIDs (TLBI ALLE1IS) when a VM is torn down, as a VMID rollover would. On the Cortex-A53, what a guest that ran with its MMU on leaves in the TLBs makes every broadcast TLB invalidation of the host slower until the next rollover (cyclictest maximum on the isolated CPU 60 -> 130-200 us under stress-ng --tlb-shootdown), and invalidating by VMID does not remove it. The patch header has the details; the measurements are in runphi_testing/runphi-kvm-rt-kria.
